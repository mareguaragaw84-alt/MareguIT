About EthioCode Training Center

EthioCode was founded in 2018 with the mission of bridging the digital skill gap in Ethiopia. We started as a small center focusing solely on web development and have since grown into a leading hub for all modern tech skills, from graphics design to mobile application development.

Our approach combines theoretical knowledge with intensive hands-on project experience, ensuring our graduates are job-ready and competitive in the global market.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To empower the next generation of Ethiopian tech professionals through practical, affordable, and world-class computer science and digital skills training.

Vision

To be the premier technological training institute in East Africa, known for producing innovative and highly skilled digital experts.
